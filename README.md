- **[Adaptive latent reasoning](https://github.com/famatodlr/adaptive-latent-reasoning)**: lets a latent chain-of-thought model decide how many hidden reasoning steps to spend on each problem, instead of the fixed number SIM-CoT uses. It explores three ways to size that budget: a classifier that predicts it up front, adaptive vectors within each step, and a PonderNet halting head that learns when to stop, all trained on GSM8K and re-validated on a held-out split. Comes with a [paper](https://github.com/famatodlr/adaptive-latent-reasoning/blob/main/report/paper.pdf) and a [poster](https://github.com/famatodlr/adaptive-latent-reasoning/blob/main/report/poster.pdf).
- **[FULBO](https://github.com/varbelaiz/FULBO) (Field Unified Localization and Ball/player Observation)**: analyzes football matches from SoccerNet broadcast footage. It detects players and referees with YOLO11 and the ball with a dedicated patch-based model, tracks every player with ByteTrack, splits them into teams by clustering DINOv2 embeddings, and fits a homography from detected pitch landmarks to project everyone onto a tactical minimap. Comes with a [paper](https://github.com/varbelaiz/FULBO/blob/main/report/FULBO-paper.pdf) and a [poster](https://github.com/varbelaiz/FULBO/blob/main/report/FULBO-poster.pdf).
- **[SUV price prediction](https://github.com/varbelaiz/suv-price-prediction)**: predicts the price of SUVs listed on MercadoLibre Argentina from about 18,000 listings. It extracts technical attributes from the free-text titles, reduces over 1,100 version names to about 200 by matching them against a scraped catalogue and clustering the rest with DBSCAN, target-encodes the high-cardinality fields, and compares linear regression, Random Forest, XGBoost and an MLP after hyperparameter search. Comes with a [report](https://github.com/varbelaiz/suv-price-prediction/blob/main/Informe.pdf).
